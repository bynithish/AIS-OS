# Signal → Pain Point → Question Map

This is the actual synthesis tool for `/draft-outreach`. `context/about-business.md` has the ICP and offer — it does not list pain points. This file does: given a raw signal from `RAW_NOTES`, what it tells you, which pain point it points to, and the diagnostic question that tests it.

**The output shape this produces is signal + one open question — not a value-prop pitch.** Nithish's one DM opener that actually got a reply (`references/voice.md` sample 2) is exactly this shape: a specific observation, then a question testing whether the pain is real. Nobody wants to be pitched cold; a question invites a reply, a pitch invites silence.

## Pain point legend

Source: agency-owner research brief, May 2026 (SparkToro, Bain, IBM, Jasper, CoSchedule, MarTech — see full citations at the end of this file). These are the real, sourced definitions — not inferred from outreach copy.

| # | Pain point | What's actually happening | Outreach tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **No one can prove AI ROI** | Only 41% of marketers can confidently point to improved ROI from AI. Teams run "productivity theatre" — tools are busy, the business isn't moving. | **Primary** — lead with this |
| 5 | **Fragmented AI tool stacks** | Agencies run 6+ AI tools with no data flow between them. Only 16% of AI initiatives have scaled org-wide. Integrated stacks get 2x the cost efficiency of disconnected ones. | **Primary** — lead with this, and the **default** when a signal doesn't clearly point elsewhere |
| 1 | **The squeeze play** | Clients see the same AI tools agencies use and ask "why the same retainer?" Sales cycles now run 7-12+ weeks as prospects do internal math. 53% of agencies now see AI as a threat to the business model (up from 44% in 2024). | Advanced/symptom — routes to #3 (the fix is proving value AI alone doesn't) |
| 2 | **Creative homogenization** | Everyone draws from the same AI training data and prompts — output looks interchangeable, RFPs get lost on genericness, and the only lever left is price. | Advanced — don't lead with it |
| 6 | **Clients going in-house** | Brands use AI to build internal capability and pull work in-house (e.g. a major beverage brand pulled media strategy, insights, and analytics entirely). Hits boutique agencies (1-10 people) hardest. | Advanced — routes to #3 |
| 4 | **Talent pipeline collapsing** | AI automates the exact tasks that trained juniors (keyword research, reporting, campaign setup) — 66% of owners worry juniors now have fewer career opportunities, which means fewer future senior handlers. | **Not cold-DM detectable** — nobody posts "worried my juniors have no future." Surface at the discovery call if it comes up organically; don't route a signal here. |

**Default to #5 unless a signal strongly points elsewhere** — it's the fastest to verify and lowest-friction to ask about. Don't lead outreach with #1, #2, or #6; if a signal maps there, use the pain point it routes to (#3) instead. #4 isn't reachable from a cold read at all — see the note above.

**Hottest convergence (per the research):** #2, #3, and #5 share one root cause — agencies have no system to make AI output distinctively theirs, prove its impact, or connect their tools. Useful for how the eventual offer gets framed once a call is booked; not something to pack into a first-touch DM.

## LinkedIn Profile / Bio Signals

**Named AI tool or service mentioned** (e.g., "using Jasper," "built a proprietary data engine")
- Learn: whether stated AI/automation use has translated into a measurable outcome
- Pain point: #3 — No ROI proof
- Question: "You're using [tool] — has it moved margins, or mostly just speed?"

**"AI-powered" / "AI-driven" positioning in services**
- Learn: whether the claim is backed by proof or is aspirational marketing language
- Pain point: #3
- Question: "You position [service] as AI-driven — speed play, or margin play?"

**Recent AI-adjacent hire** (AI engineer, automation specialist, prompt/ops role)
- Learn: whether the hire is building something new or patching an existing gap
- Pain point: #3 (can't prove ROI on the investment) or #5 (fixing fragmentation)
- Question: "Hiring for [role] — building something new, or fixing a bottleneck?"

**Heavy junior/entry-level hiring, no AI-specific roles**
- Learn: whether junior headcount is absorbing repetitive/automatable work by hand, because nothing's automated it yet
- Pain point: #5 — fragmented/manual systems *(remapped from an earlier "#4" draft — heavy manual hiring is a symptom of no automation, not the talent-pipeline pain; see legend)*
- Question: "Scaling the team up — growth, or current people maxed on day-to-day?"

**Small team, disproportionately high output/client volume**
- Learn: whether systems or sheer people-effort is carrying the load
- Pain point: #5 — either way (systems not carrying the load, or people manually carrying what systems should)
- Question: "[X] clients, team of [Y] — what's actually carrying that load?"

**Full-service agency, many disciplines under one roof** (SEO + social + paid + content + dev, etc.)
- Learn: whether tools/data connect across disciplines or are manually stitched
- Pain point: #5 — fragmented tools
- Question: "[N] disciplines under one roof — talking to each other, or stitched manually?"

**Vague/generic "about" language** ("smart solutions," "holistic," "grow together")
- Learn: whether lack of specificity reflects lack of differentiated AI/systems maturity
- Pain point: #5 (early/unorganized) or #3 (no way to prove value)
- Question: fallback — "Curious, has AI actually changed how [agency] runs day to day, or still mostly hype so far?"

**Client logos / case studies with hard numbers stated**
- Learn: whether those numbers are systematized or manually compiled each time
- Pain point: #3
- Question: "Case studies show real numbers — pulled together manually, or a live system behind them?"

**Creative-core positioning** (branding, content, social, video as core service, not one of many)
- Learn: whether their output is starting to feel interchangeable with competitors using similar AI tools
- Pain point: #2 — homogenization (advanced, not first-offer priority)
- Question: "Feels like a lot of creative work looks similar since everyone's using the same AI tools — you noticing that, or does your work still stand out?"

**Boutique agency + enterprise/large-brand clients named**
- Learn: whether any client has started pulling work in-house
- Pain point: #6 — clients going in-house (advanced, routes to #3 pitch)
- Question: "Any of your bigger clients building AI capability in-house and pulling work away, or still solid?"

**Long-tenured owner, sales/BD background, enterprise clients, high retention**
- Learn: whether clients are pushing back on retainers given visible AI cost savings
- Pain point: #1 — squeeze play (symptom, routes to #3 pitch)
- Question: "Clients pushing back on retainers now that they see AI cutting your costs, or not really hitting you yet?"

**Recent LinkedIn post on AI/branding/industry trend**
- Learn: whether stated opinion reflects lived practice or is still theoretical
- Pain point: varies — use as an entry, not a pain-point anchor itself
- Question: "Saw your post on [topic] — has that shown up in your own work yet, or still more theory at this stage?"

**Follows the same niche influencer/figure as you** (shared interest signal)
- Learn: shared context/tooling awareness — softer opener, still needs a real anchor
- Pain point: varies
- Question: "Noticed you follow [person] too — with [specific tools/parts of their workflow] in the mix, where's the manual bottleneck still sitting for you?"

## Website / Live Behavioral Signals

**Contact form present**
- Learn: what actually happens after submission — routed automatically or manually checked
- Pain point: #5
- Question: "Saw you have a contact form — what happens to it once someone submits?"

**No live chat / no immediate response mechanism**
- Learn: whether leads sit cold until manual follow-up
- Pain point: #5
- Question: "No live chat on the site — intentional, or just hasn't come up yet?"

**Active, frequently updated blog**
- Learn: who produces it and how much time it costs
- Pain point: #5 *(remapped from an earlier "#4" draft — manual content production is a systems gap, not the talent-pipeline pain; see legend)*
- Question: "Blog's pretty active — is that in-house, and who's actually writing it?"

**Blog gone stale / no recent posts**
- Learn: whether it's a deliberate deprioritization or a bandwidth problem
- Pain point: #5
- Question: "Blog's been quiet since [month] — deliberate pause, or just no time?"

**Calendly / booking link**
- Learn: whether bookings sync automatically into their pipeline/CRM
- Pain point: #5
- Question: "Booking link's set up — feeds into your pipeline automatically, or someone checks it manually?"

**Newsletter signup present**
- Learn: whether it's actually active or dormant infrastructure
- Pain point: #5 — either way (dormant system, or no automation freeing up time to run it)
- Question: "Saw the newsletter signup — is that actually running, or more dormant right now?"

**Careers/jobs page listing not on LinkedIn**
- Learn: same as LinkedIn hiring signal, catches roles not posted elsewhere
- Pain point: #3 or #5, depending on role
- Question: same structure as the LinkedIn hiring question, adapted to the specific role

**Slow load time / broken element experienced as a visitor**
- Learn: direct, lived proof of an operational gap
- Pain point: #5
- Question: "Noticed [specific thing] loads slow / isn't working — on your radar?"

## Notes on Use

- Default to #5 unless a signal strongly points elsewhere — it has the fastest, most verifiable proof and the lowest-friction ask.
- #1, #2, #6 are real but route back into #3 — don't lead outreach with them. #4 is real too but has no reliable cold-read signal; leave it for the discovery call.
- Website/behavioral signals read as less "researched," more "I engaged with your business directly" — lower friction than quoting a stat back at someone.
- One question per message. Follow their actual words for the next question — don't stack a second question in the same message.
- `RAW_NOTES` will often contain signals for more than one row in this map. Pick the strongest single match (most specific detail beats vague, named beats implied) rather than combining several into one message — the personalization bar in `SKILL.md` still applies: could this exact line have gone to 500 other people unedited?
