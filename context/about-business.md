# About the Business

**What:** An AI agency helping digital marketing agencies (DMAs) become AI-native — higher client capacity, higher margins, faster delivery. Positioning is aspirational without a track record yet, so the actual go-to-market is a low-risk "proof of value" engagement, not the big vision pitch.

**ICP:** Digital marketing agencies, ~5-50 employees. 500+ first-degree LinkedIn connections with DMA owners already exist (accepted requests, no real conversations yet) — this is the initial outreach pool. Niche chosen from direct prior experience running a full-service DMA, plus market research suggesting marketing services are unusually well-positioned to go AI-native (sell shovels, don't compete as another agency).

**The offer** (mechanics confirmed `brainstorms/2026-09-15-voice-and-self-presentation.md`, Q10 — corrects pricing detail vs. an earlier draft in `raw/dm-writing-context.md`):
1. Free 45-min discovery call → pre-book a follow-up call for the next day.
2. Using an AI-consultant Claude skill, find one high-ROI, low-risk opportunity (lowest-hanging fruit) in their business.
3. Come back within 24 hours with findings and a paid offer: a 2-week pilot, priced at **10% of what it saves the agency annually**, **50% upfront**.
4. If it doesn't work after the initial 2 weeks, keep working for a **second 2-week extension** (not an immediate refund) — full refund only if it still doesn't work after that extension. Real risk-reversal window is up to 4 weeks of work, not a flat 2 weeks.
5. If it delivers, upsell to a retainer: ongoing identification, implementation, and management of AI-powered improvements across the agency.

**Revenue model:** Pre-revenue — no clients yet. Expected payment rails: Google Pay, Wise, or bank transfer. Planned tracking: QuickBooks.

**Credibility gap:** No case studies, no first client yet. Plan is to close that gap by building in public — a personal brand that documents the build itself (see `context/priorities.md` for the content system), making Nithish his own case study.
